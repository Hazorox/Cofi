Cozy Cafe SFX — 46 soft sound effects for café, bakery and cooking games

Coffee and tea, kitchen and bakery, shop, UI, jingles and seamless ambience loops, with a
soft, warm sound that fits cozy games.

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit. Sound effects are mono and start and end in silence; the
ambience loops are stereo and repeat with no gap or click (set your player to loop).
